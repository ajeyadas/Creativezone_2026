Intensive Masterclasses & Specialized Workshops

Deep-dive weekend and seasonal bootcamps for dedicated students and teen artists seeking advanced technique refinement.

Module 01 2-Day Weekend Intensive

Mastering Light & Shadow

Charcoal and graphite value studies.

Students master the classical 9-step value scale, separating light family from shadow family using vine charcoal, compressed charcoal, and kneaded erasers on toned paper.

Workshop Curriculum Breakdown
  • Day 1: Highlight, halftone, core shadow, reflected light & cast shadow geometry.
  • Day 2: Classical drapery folds & plaster cast still-life rendering on archival Ingres paper.
Module 02 Seasonal Outdoor Bootcamp

Plein Air & Urban Sketching

Fast perspective and live environmental capture.

Stepping outside the studio with travel sketchbooks, waterproof ink pens, and portable watercolor pans to capture architecture, trees, and shifting daylight in real time.

Workshop Curriculum Breakdown
  • Day 1: Eye-level horizon lines, 1-point & 2-point architectural perspective & rapid thumbnailing.
  • Day 2: Expressive ink line weight, atmospheric washes & capturing foliage textures outdoors.
Module 03 3-Day Studio Masterclass

Oil & Acrylic Painting Intensive

Canvas preparation, layering, and impasto techniques.

A comprehensive immersion into professional easel painting—from priming stretched cotton canvas and toned underpainting to expressive brushwork and palette knife texture.

Workshop Curriculum Breakdown
  • Session A: Surface preparation, burnt sienna imprimatura & monochromatic value block-in.
  • Session B: Color temperature shifts, optical glazing & sculptural palette-knife impasto.
Module 04 1-on-1 & Small Cohort

Portfolio Review & Exhibition Prep

Curating a cohesive body of work for admissions and competitions.

Tailored for ambitious teen artists preparing applications for design/art colleges, magnet schools, IB/IGCSE art assessments, or national youth exhibitions.

Workshop Curriculum Breakdown
  • Part 1: Auditing existing pieces, identifying skill gaps & sequencing 12–20 cohesive works.
  • Part 2: Writing a compelling Artist Statement, high-resolution photographing & mock critique.

01. Artistic Journey & Background

Trained in classical observational drawing, design, and contemporary painting, Rituparna Das has spent the past 15–20 years creating and teaching fine art. Her work has been featured across exhibitions, private collections, and creative showcases.

Alongside her active studio practice, she discovered early on that teaching young minds was not separate from her art—it was an extension of it. Over the last decade and a half, she has mentored hundreds of children from their very first pastel strokes at age three to scholarship-winning university portfolios at sixteen.

02. Teaching Philosophy

Many art classes fall into one of two extremes: rigid, joyless copying that drains a child’s spirit, or unstructured craft time where students never learn how to see or render what they imagine.

Our philosophy centers on patience, individual pacing, and foundational technique without stifling raw childhood creativity. We never draw over a student’s work or rush them to match a template. Instead, we teach them how to observe light, mix colors intentionally, and trust their own hand—so technical skill becomes a springboard for personal expression.

03. The Studio Environment

Our atelier is designed as a calm, inspiring sanctuary away from screens and academic pressure—a safe, encouraging space equipped with quality materials and abundant natural lighting where young artists feel respected as real creators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are art supplies and studio materials included? +

Students would need to bring their own oil pastels, watercolor pans & any additional accessories that's required

Does my child need prior drawing experience to join? +

Not at all. Because our batches are capped at 6 to 8 students, our mentor adapts demonstrations to each student’s starting point while keeping them with peers of their own age group.

How does the introductory trial session work? +

During a 45-minute trial session, your child experiences a guided mini-project alongside our mentor, and parents receive a personalized curriculum walkthrough and readiness recommendation.

How often are student exhibitions held? +

We host our Annual Fine Art Student Showcase every Spring, along with seasonal parent open-studio days at the conclusion of each term.
